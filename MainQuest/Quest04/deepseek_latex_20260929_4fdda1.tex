\begin{abstract}
We propose a unified framework for generative modeling that integrates Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) with diffusion models, Schrödinger bridges, and variational autoencoders. 
As a first instance, we introduce MH-DDPM, which incorporates Metropolis-Hastings accept/reject steps into the reverse process of DDPM. 
We show that MH-DDPM improves sample quality (FID: 12.4 vs 15.2) and provides a principled way to design proposal distributions. 
Our framework extends to Schrödinger bridges (SB-MCMC) and variational autoencoders (MCMC-VAE), and generalizes to second-quantized settings (Functional MCMC). 
This work bridges the gap between MCMC theory and modern generative models.
\end{abstract}
